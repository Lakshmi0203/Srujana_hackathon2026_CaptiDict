02 · SCREENS & ACTIONS

What each part of CaptiDict does

▦
OVERVIEW

Your current position

Current cash is the opening balance. Available cash is projected day-30 cash. Net cash is the projected change. Runway is the first day the balance goes below zero—or 30+ days if it does not. Alerts and Cash Health compare the lowest balance with the ₹1,50,000 reserve.

The cash bridge explains opening cash, confidence-weighted receipts and full scheduled payments. The scenario tabs compare the base case, a late-customer stress case, and that stress case with a supplier extension.

⌁
FORECAST

See the path and test changes

The chart shows the expected balance, an active what-if line, and the reserve. The warning calls out the lowest point and its date. The action planner suggests three locally calculated next steps; checking them off saves that progress on this device.

Controls can delay the largest pending receipt by 0–14 days, add a ₹0–₹2,50,000 one-off cost on a selected date, or reduce pending sales inflows by 0–80%. Compare this move shifts the sample supplier payment by 14 days.

For a natural-language scenario, try “Aster pays 20 days late and sales drop 10%”. Preview and confirm the assumptions before applying them. The local parser needs a customer name that appears in a pending receipt; it makes no AI/API request. It accepts receipt delays up to 90 days, sales reductions up to 100%, and unexpected expenses up to ₹1 crore.

The Forecast backtest panel reads a CSV containing historical forecast date, target date, predicted low/high, and actual cash. Use YYYY-MM-DD dates and plain signed INR numbers without thousands separators. It calculates in-band coverage and median band width locally. At least 20 observations must cover the six-month window, including a recent snapshot, before the result is labeled ready. This prototype currently plots an expected line only; it does not generate or retain historical prediction bands, and no sample accuracy is presented as real.

⇄
TRANSACTIONS

Maintain the inputs

Use All transactions to add an inflow or outflow, review its date and amount, and mark it Received or Paid only after it has happened. Undo returns it to Forecast and reverses the balance adjustment.

Open Receivables for the Smart Chase List. It ranks pending customer receipts using amount, your confidence input, and the expected date. Select a receipt, choose Friendly, Firm, or Final reminder, edit the local template, and copy it yourself. The draft does not assert a contractual due date or send a message; projected sales are excluded.

CSV import previews rows locally, flags invalid or duplicate entries, and adds only valid forecast rows. Export downloads the current daily forecast and transaction list as CSV. Nothing is uploaded.

₹
PAY-ORDER PLANNER

Prioritize bills when cash is tight

On Forecast, enter your estimated cost of a one-week delay for each payment and tick Can discuss date only after checking its terms. The planner ranks higher cost per ₹1,000 earlier for payment. When the modeled low is below reserve, it tests eligible bills from the lowest delay cost upward using a 14-day shift.

Apply comparison changes the forecast scenario only; it does not change transaction dates or authorize late payment. Demo estimates are fictional. Confirm penalties, agreements, payroll and statutory deadlines before acting.

₹
FUNDING READINESS

Estimate a reserve buffer

At the end of Forecast, the funding brief estimates the extra cash needed to keep the lowest modeled balance at or above your reserve in 95% of 500 repeatable paths. Receipts are sampled as arriving in full according to their confidence; scheduled payments count in full. The brief reflects the currently selected scenario and includes the pending inputs, expected low, P05 low, and any backtest data currently loaded.

Choose Export PDF brief to open a local report, then use the browser’s Print dialog and select Save as PDF. It makes no bank or eligibility claim. Replace demo entries with verified records and review the assumptions with a CA or lender.

✦

The chat is not saved. Use Clear saved demo data to restore the sample data and clear local workspace changes.

03 · HOW FLOWSETU PREDICTS

A transparent, date-based estimate

There is no hidden prediction model or bank feed. The chart is calculated from the cash balance, dated transactions, and scenario controls currently saved in this browser.

Projected closing cash = opening cash + expected receipts − scheduled payments
01
Start from current cash

The value set in Overview is the opening balance for the first forecast day.

02
Weight expected receipts

For the standard forecast, each pending inflow contributes amount × confidence. For example, ₹1,00,000 at 80% contributes ₹80,000 to the expected line.

03
Count scheduled payments in full

Every pending outflow reduces cash by its full amount. Its confidence is not used.

04
Apply movements on their dates

CaptiDict builds 31 daily points across the 30-day view. Transactions on the same date are netted together because this prototype has no intraday timing.

05
Find the low and compare the reserve

The lowest daily balance determines the warning. Below ₹0 is a possible shortfall; below ₹1,50,000 is a reserve breach; up to ₹60,000 above reserve is a watch-out.

06
Recalculate what-if cases

Scenario changes adjust dates or amounts, then rerun the same date-based calculation. The chart, ending cash, lowest point, and alerts update from those assumptions.

₹
What “Safe to spend” means here

The app runs 500 repeatable sample paths. Each pending receipt arrives in a path according to its entered confidence; outflows are included in full. It takes the fifth-percentile (lower 5%) of each path’s lowest balance, subtracts the ₹1,50,000 reserve, then rounds down to the nearest ₹500 (never below zero). This is a conservative model estimate over the displayed 30-day horizon—not a guarantee that spending is safe.

P95
How the funding buffer is estimated

The report uses the same 500 local paths. It takes the lower 5th-percentile of each path’s lowest balance, subtracts that from the reserve, and rounds the shortfall up to ₹500. The displayed path count shows how many simulations remain above reserve after adding that estimate. This is scenario math based on the entered confidence assumptions—not a guarantee of real-world 95% safety or a credit-line recommendation.

Confidence is your input, not a learned probability. The model assumes receipt outcomes are independent, uses fixed sample dates and a fixed reserve, and does not predict from history.

04 · PRIVACY, RESET & LIMITS

Know where the demo stops

Local by design

Transactions, settings, and checklist progress are saved in this browser’s local storage. The demo makes no bank connection and sends no data to an AI service or analytics endpoint.

Not encrypted or authenticated

The login is only a local demo gate. Browser local storage is not encrypted; anyone using the same browser profile may be able to inspect saved data. Use fictional data only.

Planning aid, not advice

Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees or accounting records. Confirm amounts, receipt dates, and payment obligations before acting. This prototype does not provide tax, lending, investment, or legal advice.

Restore or clear

Next 30 days restores the fictional sample. Clear saved demo data in Private AI also restores the sample and removes saved workspace edits from local storage.
